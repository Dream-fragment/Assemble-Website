## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Assemble Notes. We build specialized desktop and web-based note-taking tools designed for mathematics, allowing you to seamlessly create and organize notes using LaTeX formulas and interactive GeoGebra graphs. Because our free version stores notes locally on your device and our Pro version offers optional cloud synchronization, we have designed our privacy practices to be as lightweight and respectful as possible.

We believe that your notes are your private thoughts and intellectual property. This Privacy Policy explains in plain language how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website and application. It also outlines your rights under global privacy laws, including the GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, and US State Privacy Laws (such as the CCPA/CPRA).

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, please reach out to us at **support@assemblenotes.com**.

## 2. What We Collect & Why

We only collect information that we actually need to provide and improve Assemble Notes.

### Information You Give Us Directly

We process this data primarily on the legal basis of fulfilling our contract with you.

- **Account Information:** We collect your email address and a password to create and secure your account.
- **Payment Information:** If you subscribe to Assemble Notes Pro, you provide billing details. This is processed directly by our payment provider (Stripe); we do not store your full credit card number.
- **Communication Records:** When you email our support team, we collect the contents of your message to help resolve your issue.
- **Account Preferences:** We save your app settings (like skin and font preferences) to ensure a consistent experience.

### Information Collected Automatically

To keep our application secure and understand how it performs, we automatically collect certain technical data. We process this based on our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving our software.

- **Device Information:** We collect basic details about the device and browser you use to access Assemble Notes, such as your operating system, to optimize the LaTeX and GeoGebra rendering for your specific setup.
- **Usage Analytics:** We look at aggregated data on how features are used (e.g., how often cloud sync is triggered) so we know where to focus our development efforts.
- **Technical Logs:** We collect error logs and crash reports to identify and fix bugs.
- **Local Storage:** We use essential local storage to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Every piece of information we gather serves a specific purpose:

- **Core Service Delivery:** We use your email to authenticate your account and, if you are a Pro user, we use our infrastructure to securely synchronize, backup, and maintain version history of your notes across your devices. *(Legal basis: Contract)*
- **Service Improvement:** We analyze technical logs and usage data to make our block-based editor faster and more reliable. *(Legal basis: Legitimate Interest)*
- **Communication:** We use your email to send important account updates, security alerts, and billing receipts. *(Legal basis: Contract)*
- **Marketing:** If you opt in, we may send you newsletters about new features. You can opt out of these at any time. *(Legal basis: Consent)*
- **Security and Fraud Prevention:** We monitor account access patterns to detect unauthorized logins and protect your data. *(Legal basis: Legitimate Interest)*
- **Legal Compliance:** We maintain certain financial records to comply with tax and accounting laws. *(Legal basis: Legal Obligation)*

## 5. Your Privacy Rights

No matter where you live, we believe you should have full control over your personal data. You have the right to:

- **Access Your Data:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. We will provide this within 30 days.
- **Correct Inaccuracies:** Update your email, password, and billing information directly in your account settings.
- **Delete Your Data:** Delete your account at any time. Doing so will permanently erase your personal data and cloud-synced notes from our active servers.
- **Port Your Data:** Export your notes from the application in standard formats at any time.
- **Opt-Out of Marketing:** Every marketing email we send contains a one-click unsubscribe link.
- **Withdraw Consent:** If you previously gave us consent to process your data, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting the legality of our past processing.
- **Lodge a Complaint:** If you feel we have mishandled your data, you have the right to complain to your relevant data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please email **support@assemblenotes.com**.

## 6. Data Security

We take the security of your mathematical work seriously. We implement industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest. Access to our production servers is strictly limited and protected by multi-factor authentication.

However, we want to be honest: no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

In the unlikely event of a data breach that compromises your personal information, we commit to notifying you and the relevant authorities within 72 hours of becoming aware of the incident.

## 7. Data Retention

We keep your data only as long as necessary to provide our services.

- **Active Account Data:** We retain your account information and Pro cloud-synced notes until you delete your account. Once you trigger an account deletion, your data is removed from our active systems immediately.
- **Financial Records:** We are legally required to retain basic transaction history (which does not include full credit card numbers) for up to 7 years for tax and accounting purposes.
- **Technical Logs:** System and error logs are routinely purged and are kept for no longer than 12 months.
- **Marketing Preferences:** We retain your marketing opt-in status until you withdraw it.

## 19. Controller and Processor Roles

Privacy laws distinguish between the "Controller" (the entity that decides why and how data is processed) and the "Processor" (the entity that processes data on behalf of the Controller).

When you use Assemble Notes:

- We act as a **Data Processor** for the actual content of the notes, LaTeX formulas, and GeoGebra graphs you create. **You own this data.**
- We act as a **Data Controller** for the account information, billing details, and usage analytics we collect to manage your subscription and operate our business.

For business and enterprise customers, these roles and our specific obligations are further detailed in our **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)**, which is available upon request.
